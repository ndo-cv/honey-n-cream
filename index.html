01 Our Story

A cosy corner
of the High Road

Honey & Cream is a relaxed, friendly café built around the simple pleasures of brunch done properly — soft scrambled eggs, good bread, carefully pulled coffee and something sweet to finish. It's the kind of place you can bring the whole family, settle in with a book on your own, or catch up with friends over a long breakfast.

Our kitchen moves from hearty breakfast plates through brunch classics, fresh salads and bowls, right through to indulgent pancakes, milkshakes and a thoughtful drinks list — all served in a casual, trendy, genuinely cosy room with a warm welcome for dogs and children alike.
